Light Read English — Privacy Policy
Effective date: 2026-10-21
Developer: Light Read English (Wattflow)
Contact: robin421@gmail.com

1. Overview
Light Read English is an English reading and grammar learning app. We do not require an account. This policy explains what data the app processes, why, and your choices.

2. Data stored on your device
Books, imported files, saved words and sentences, reading progress, settings, practice records and grammar learning records are stored locally on your device. We do not upload your library to our servers. You can delete this data in the app or by uninstalling the app.

3. Data we collect automatically
We use Google Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Performance, and PostHog to understand app usage, diagnose crashes and improve performance. These services may collect app activity, device/app information, device or other identifiers, diagnostics, crash logs and performance data. Data is transmitted over HTTPS.

4. Data you provide or send for features
When you use translation, dictionary lookup, grammar analysis or web import, the relevant text, sentence or URL may be sent to our backend (er-api.wattflow.net) and/or third-party service providers (for example Google/Bing/gtx/MyMemory translation, AI analysis providers) to return the result. Do not submit sensitive personal information. We do not use this content for advertising.
If you contact customer support, your messages and contact details are processed by our support provider (Knocket) to answer you.

5. Third-party services
Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Performance, Remote Config), PostHog, translation/AI providers, Knocket customer support, and the app backend. Their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policies.

6. Purposes and legal bases
We use data to provide and improve the app, diagnose problems, secure the service and comply with law. Where required, we rely on our legitimate interests, your consent, or performance of a contract.

7. Sharing
We do not sell your personal data. We share data with service providers that help us operate the app, when required by law, or to protect rights and safety.

8. Retention
Local data remains until you delete it. Analytics and crash data are retained according to the providers' settings and our operational needs. Support messages are retained as needed to handle your request.

9. Security
We use HTTPS for network requests and take reasonable measures to protect data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

10. Children
The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child provided data.

11. Your rights
Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, and to data portability. You can clear local data in the app or uninstall it. For requests, contact robin421@gmail.com. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

12. AI disclaimer
Translations, dictionary definitions and grammar analyses may be generated or assisted by AI and may contain errors. They are for language learning reference only and are not professional advice. Please verify important information.

13. Copyright and content
The app includes public-domain and licensed content. You are responsible for content you import. To report content, use the in-app report feature or contact us.

14. Changes
We may update this policy. The effective date above will change; continued use means you accept the updated policy.

15. Contact
robin421@gmail.com
